About

I'm Tharun Kumar Korine Palli, an AI researcher based in Würzburg, Germany. My work sits at the intersection of research and real-world application. I'm particularly interested in how we can make AI systems more practical, efficient, and accessible.

My journey started with mechanical engineering before I discovered my passion for AI. This unique background gives me a different perspective on building systems. I think about hardware constraints, practical deployment, and real-world impact from day one.

These days, I spend most of my time working with large language models, RAG systems, computer vision, and figuring out how to deploy AI on edge devices. I enjoy the challenge of taking complex models and making them work in production environments.

What I'm doing now

AI Researcher

@ TTZ Kitzingen

I'm part of the research team at the Technology Transfer Center Kitzingen (TTZ-KT), an initiative of THWS focused on bringing AI, robotics, and digital transformation to regional industry. We work closely with local companies to solve real engineering challenges using cutting-edge AI.
